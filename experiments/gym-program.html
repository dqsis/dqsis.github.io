Back & grip
  • The trap bar deadlift replaces the conventional deadlift — the torso stays more upright and lumbar shear is lower, so the same load costs the back less.
  • There is one heavy hinge per week. Hip thrusts and back extensions build the posterior chain with little or no spinal load, and chest-supported rows replace barbell rows.
  • Straps on deadlift top sets and heavy pulls, so the arms are never the limit. Farmer's carries train grip on its own terms.
  • Leave one or two reps in reserve on the main lifts — no grinding to failure.

Retired from the previous block: face pulls, single-leg RDL, conventional deadlift, barbell row.

Cardio · winter
  • Wednesday, fixed: swimming, 45 minutes.
  • Fallback: if the pool hasn't happened by Wednesday evening, 30–40 minutes of incline treadmill walking or bike at the gym. The fallback is not skipping.
  • Saturday, optional: a run, a swim, or a long walk — whichever is most appealing. If it's skipped, the week still counts.
