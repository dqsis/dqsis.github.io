The idea
An experiment in building a capsule wardrobe — a small, deliberate set of pieces that carries a full week at the office and works just as well off the clock. It runs on twelve items for now, and that number will shift over time, but the intent stays the same: fewer, better things. High-quality pieces, one less decision every morning, and a look that stays polished without losing something modern and a little playful.
